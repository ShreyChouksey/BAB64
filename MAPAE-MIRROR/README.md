# MAPAE Research Mirror

This directory is an authorized public mirror of the **Million-Address Perpetual Adversarial Exposure Experiment (MAPAE)** operated by Shrey Chouksey.

MAPAE concerns a fixed corpus of **1,000,000 Bitcoin mainnet native-SegWit (`bc1q...`) public addresses**. The experiment is designed for lawful, long-lived adversarial scrutiny rather than privacy.

## Critical notice

This mirror contains **public Bitcoin addresses only**. It contains no private keys, WIFs, seeds, mnemonics, entropy source, extended private keys, recovery material, signing secrets, or equivalent confidential material.

Researchers may index, validate, compare, statistically analyze, inspect blockchain history, and report reproducible weaknesses. This experiment does not authorize unauthorized access, malware, phishing, credential theft, social engineering, denial-of-service activity, spam, fake accounts, fabricated commerce, circumvention of service rules, or attacks against unrelated third parties.

**Maximum exposure. Maximum longevity. Maximum independent scrutiny. Zero private-key disclosure. Zero unauthorized activity. Zero spam. Zero deception. Zero circumvention.**
